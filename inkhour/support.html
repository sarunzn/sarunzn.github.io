Support

How can we help?

Most questions are answered below. If yours isn't, open an issue on GitHub and include your iPhone model and iOS version. Issues are public, so leave out anything personal.

Starting and stopping the timer

Start a timer from the bar at the bottom of the app, the Timer widget, or the Inkhour control in Control Center or on the Action Button. Stop it from any of those, or from the Live Activity on your Lock Screen. When it stops, the session becomes a block on the day's grid, rounded to your square size, and Inkhour asks how it went if the label counts things.

The Live Activity disappeared

iOS ends Live Activities after about 8 hours. Your timer keeps running. Open Inkhour and the Live Activity comes back.

I'm not getting reminders

Turn on Remind me to fill in my day in Inkhour's Settings and pick a time. Then check that notifications are on for Inkhour in Settings → Notifications → Inkhour.

Moving to a new iPhone

Inkhour doesn't sync through iCloud. If you move to a new iPhone with Quick Start or an iCloud backup, Inkhour comes along with everything in it. Otherwise, in Inkhour's Settings tap Save a backup… and save the file to iCloud Drive. On the new iPhone, tap Have a backup? Restore it on the first screen, or Restore from a backup… in Settings.

I deleted something by mistake

Inkhour saves a backup each day and keeps the last 14 in the Files app under On My iPhone › Inkhour › Backups. In Inkhour's Settings, tap Restore from a backup… and pick the day you want. Restoring replaces what's in the app now with what's in the backup.

Removing the sample month

In Settings, tap Remove sample blocks. Only the made-up blocks go; everything you logged yourself stays.

Privacy

Inkhour has no accounts, ads or analytics, and your notebook stays on your iPhone. Read the Privacy Policy.

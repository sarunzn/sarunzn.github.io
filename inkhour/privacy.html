Privacy Policy

Your notebook stays on your iPhone.

Inkhour is a time tracker that looks like graph paper: you color in how you spent your day and count what you got done. This policy explains what information the current version uses and where it is stored. Inkhour has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, does not connect to the internet, and does not sell or share your information.

Effective October 28, 2026

Information you add

Inkhour stores what you put into it: your labels and sublabels and their colors, the blocks of time you log or time with the timer, the counts you add to them (such as questions and how many you got right, cards, pages, tasks, distance or pomodoros), your goals, your settings and any timer that is running. All of it is stored on your iPhone. Inkhour and its widgets share it through an Apple App Group container on your device so the widgets, the Control Center control and the Live Activity can show and start your timer.

If you choose the sample month during setup, Inkhour fills in made-up blocks so you can look around. They are marked as samples and can be removed at any time in Settings.

No servers, no sync

Inkhour does not send your information to the developer or to any other service, and it does not sync through iCloud. Like other apps, its data is included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer) if you have them turned on. Those backups are made and held by Apple or on your computer, under your control.

Backups and exports

Once a day, Inkhour saves a backup file of your labels, blocks, counts and settings in the Files app under On My iPhone › Inkhour › Backups, and keeps the last 14. These files stay on your iPhone.

When you save a backup or export your entries as a spreadsheet, Inkhour creates the file on your device and hands it to the place you choose, such as iCloud Drive or the share sheet. Where it goes next is up to you. When you restore a backup, Inkhour reads only the file you pick.

Reminders and Live Activities

The daily "fill in your day" reminder and the warning when a timer runs long are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent through a server. The daily reminder is off until you turn it on, and you can turn notifications off at any time in the Settings app. While a timer runs, Inkhour shows a Live Activity on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, which you can also turn off in the Settings app.

Deleting your information

You can delete a block, a label or every entry from inside Inkhour. Deleting the app removes everything Inkhour stored on your iPhone, including the daily backups in its Backups folder. Backup files you saved somewhere else, such as iCloud Drive, stay there until you delete them.

Children

Inkhour does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

Changes

If a future version handles information differently, for example by adding iCloud sync or an online feature, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so leave out anything personal.

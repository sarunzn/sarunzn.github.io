Support

How can we help?

Most questions are answered below. If yours isn't, open an issue on GitHub and include your iPhone model and iOS version. Issues are public, so leave out anything personal.

A receipt was read wrong

Keepsake always shows the details it read before saving, so you can fix anything it got wrong. Flat, well-lit receipts read best. Use Scan Receipt rather than a photo when you can, because the scanner straightens the page. On devices without Apple Intelligence, Keepsake can fill in fewer details automatically.

I'm not getting reminders

Check that notifications are on for Keepsake in Settings → Notifications → Keepsake. Reminders arrive at 9 AM: a few days before the return deadline, on the last day to return, and before a warranty ends. A purchase with no return window or warranty set has no reminders.

My purchases aren't syncing

Make sure every device is signed in to the same Apple Account, iCloud Drive is on, and you have free iCloud storage. Open Keepsake on each device and give it a moment; large receipt photos can take longer to arrive.

Restoring Keepsake Pro

Keepsake Pro is a one-time purchase with no subscription. On a new device, open Settings in Keepsake and tap Restore Purchases. Pro is tied to the Apple Account you bought it with, and Family Sharing is supported.

A purchase is in the wrong category

Open the purchase, tap Edit and pick the right category. Keepsake remembers your choice and sorts similar purchases the same way from then on.

Privacy

Keepsake has no accounts, ads or analytics. Read the Privacy Policy.

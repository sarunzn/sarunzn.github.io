Privacy Policy

Your receipts stay yours.

Keepsake keeps track of what you bought and when its return window and warranty end. This policy explains what information the current version uses and where it is stored. Keepsake has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not sell or share your information.

Effective October 27, 2026

Information you add

Keepsake stores the purchases you add: item name, store, price and currency, purchase date, return window, warranty length, notes, and any receipt photos along with the text read from them. This information is stored on your device. Keepsake and its Home Screen widget share it through an Apple App Group container on your device so the widget can show upcoming deadlines.

iCloud sync

When you are signed in to iCloud, Keepsake stores your purchases and receipt photos in your private iCloud database so they appear on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. This data is held by Apple under your account, and the developer of Keepsake cannot see or access it. Sync depends on iCloud being available and having enough iCloud storage. If you are not signed in to iCloud, Keepsake keeps everything on your device only.

Reading receipts on your device

When you scan a receipt with the camera or choose a photo, Keepsake reads the text with Apple's Vision framework and, on devices that support Apple Intelligence, uses Apple's on-device language model to fill in the purchase details. All of this happens on your device. Keepsake does not send receipt photos or their text to the developer or to any other service.

Keepsake also sorts purchases into categories and writes a short summary of your spending on the Insights screen. Both happen on your device, using the categories you have chosen before, simple keyword rules and, when available, Apple's on-device language model. Nothing is sent off your device to do this.

The camera is used only while you are scanning. When you choose a photo, Keepsake can access only the photo you pick, not the rest of your photo library.

Reminders

Return and warranty reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They are not sent through a server. You can turn notifications off at any time in the Settings app.

Purchases

Keepsake Pro is a one-time purchase sold through the App Store and processed by Apple. Keepsake receives the StoreKit transaction information needed to confirm that Pro is unlocked and checks it on your device. It stores whether Pro is active in the App Group container so the widget can read it. Keepsake never receives your payment details.

Exports

If you export a purchase as a PDF or your purchases as a spreadsheet, Keepsake creates the file on your device and hands it to the share sheet. Where it goes next is up to you.

Deleting your information

Deleting a purchase in Keepsake removes it, its receipt photos and its reminders from your device and, when sync is on, from your private iCloud database. Deleting the app removes the data stored on that device. To remove data stored in iCloud, delete your purchases in the app before deleting it, or manage Keepsake's iCloud data from your Apple Account settings on iPhone.

Children

Keepsake does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

Changes

If a future version handles information differently, for example by adding analytics or an online feature, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so leave out anything personal.
